# Cosmetic Ingredient Function & Combination Matrix

## Primary Cosmetic Actives

| Active Ingredient | Primary Cosmetic Mechanism | Ideal Product Format | Ideal For | Caution / Do Not Combine Directly With |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Niacinamide (2–5%)** | Supports barrier, regulates visible sebum, evens tone | Water-based serum, light cream | Oily, combination, visible pores | High-strength Vitamin C in high doses if skin is sensitive |
| **Hyaluronic Acid (1–2%)**| Multi-molecular surface hydration, plumping | Hydrating serum, gel-cream | Dehydrated, all skin types | Apply to damp skin, not bone-dry skin in arid climates |
| **Salicylic Acid (0.5–2%)**| BHA oil-soluble pore unclogging, dead skin exfoliation | Foaming cleanser, liquid exfoliant | Blemish-prone, congested skin | Do not combine in same routine step with Retinol or strong AHAs |
| **Ceramides (1–3%)** | Replenishes barrier lipids, reduces TEWL | Rich moisturizer, barrier cream | Dry, compromised, sensitive | Pairs well with all ingredients |
| **Vitamin C (10–15%)** | Free radical defense, radiance enhancement | Morning serum | Dullness, uneven tone | Do not combine directly with Retinol (use Vit C morning, Retinol night) |
| **Retinol (0.1–0.3%)** | Supports natural surface turnover, smooths texture | Evening serum or night cream | Uneven texture, visible fine lines | Night use only; never use without daytime broad-spectrum SPF |

---

## Step Combination Safety Guide

1. **Morning Routine Best Practice:**
   * Cleanser (Gentle hydrating or mild foaming)
   * Hydration / Antioxidant (Hyaluronic Acid or Vitamin C)
   * Moisturizer (Lightweight barrier lotion)
   * Sunscreen (SPF 30–50 PA+++ broad-spectrum) - Non-negotiable

2. **Evening Routine Best Practice:**
   * Cleanser (Removes daily dirt and sunscreen)
   * Targeted Care (Niacinamide or Salicylic Acid or Retinol — **choose ONE primary active per night for beginners**)
   * Moisturizer (Nourishing barrier support)
